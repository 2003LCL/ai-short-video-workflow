# Product Notes

## Target User

Small local merchants who want short-video content but lack editing skill, content planning, and consistent execution.

## First Wedge

Start with operator-assisted video production instead of a fully self-serve SaaS.

The operator keeps control of:

- topic selection
- quality control
- compliance review
- customer communication
- final publishing decision

The system automates:

- script drafting
- storyboard generation
- caption generation
- voiceover text export
- rough preview rendering

## Output Principle

The output should be treated as a generated video, not as a mock Douyin/TikTok/Shorts interface.

Preview renders should prioritize:

- visual composition
- typography
- pacing
- brand feel
- subtitle readability
- final export quality

They should avoid platform UI elements such as progress bars, platform labels, like/comment/share controls, or timecode overlays unless those elements are part of the actual video concept.

## Recommended First Verticals

- dental / clinic education content
- beauty and wellness
- food and beverage
- home repair
- local education and training

## Medical Content Boundary

For medical or health-related content, the system should avoid:

- guaranteed treatment outcomes
- exaggerated claims
- patient testimonials without proper authorization
- anxiety-driven copywriting
- specific diagnosis or treatment instructions

Human review remains mandatory before publishing.
